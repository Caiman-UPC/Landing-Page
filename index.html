Solutions

Specialized Solutions for the Highway Construction Sector

Comprehensive ecosystem engineered for road builders, concessionaires, and environmental consultants committed to operational excellence.

Air Quality & Dust Control

Optical telemetry sensors for particulate matter PM10 / PM2.5 with automated alert triggering and instant misting activation on haul roads.

ECA Air Standard • NIST Calibrated

Acoustic Pressure & Noise Monitoring

Continuous sound level meter telemetry in decibels (dBA) across day/night shifts with geo-synchronized alerts in rock drilling and blasting zones.

IEC 61672 Class 1 Standard • Digital dBA Log

Water Resources Supervision

Submersible multiparameter probes in stream crossings, river diversions, and drainage outfalls with real-time telemetry for pH, turbidity (NTU), dissolved oxygen, and hydrocarbon traces.

Clean Water Standard • Continuous Monitoring

Ground Vibration Monitoring

Ground-vibration sensors track the peak vibration level generated by heavy machinery and compaction works, with instant alerts when configured limits are exceeded.

Configurable Thresholds • 24/7 Logging

About EcoRoad Solution

Oversee multiple road concession contracts in an interactive satellite map interface (Shapefiles / GeoJSON). View active work fronts and critical points in real time with automated color-coded thresholds.

Predictive algorithms evaluating hourly trends and triggering instant notifications via SMS, enterprise WhatsApp, and email to section supervisors whenever environmental parameters reach 85% of regulatory thresholds.

Immutable vault storage with RFC 3161-compliant trusted timestamping. Every telemetry reading, geo-tagged photo, and laboratory certificate is anchored with forensic metadata (UTM WGS84 coordinates, altitude, inspector, and cryptographic signature).
